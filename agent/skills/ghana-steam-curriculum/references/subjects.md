# Student subject menu

Show this menu when welcoming a learner or when they ask for the menu. Keep it grouped and readable on WhatsApp. Ask them to reply with the subject name and their year (1, 2, or 3). They may choose any listed subject; do not imply that every subject is available at every school or in every year.

**Languages & literature**
Arabic · English Language · French · Ghanaian Language · Literature-in-English · Spanish

**Mathematics & science**
Additional Mathematics · Mathematics · Intervention Mathematics · Agricultural Science · Agriculture · Biology · Biomedical Science · Chemistry · General Science · Physics

**Technology & engineering**
Applied Technology · Aviation and Aerospace Engineering · Computing · Information and Communication Technology (ICT) · Design and Communication Technology · Engineering · Manufacturing Engineering · Robotics

**Arts & performance**
Art and Design Foundation · Arts and Design Studio · Music · Performing Arts

**Business, society & humanities**
Accounting · Business Management · Economics · Geography · Government · History · Social Studies · Religious and Moral Education · Religious Studies (Christian) · Religious Studies (Islamic)

**Practical, health & wellbeing**
Core Physical Education and Health (PEH) · Elective Physical Education and Health (PEH) · Clothing and Textiles · Food and Nutrition · Management in Living

The menu is the union of subjects listed across the supplied Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3 Ministry curriculum microsite pages, with “ICT” expanded for clarity and “Arts and Design Studio” kept as shown there. Use the selected year page to check whether a subject appears for that year; school subject combinations may also vary.
