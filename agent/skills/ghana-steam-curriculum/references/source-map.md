# Curriculum source map

## Ghana Ministry of Education and NaCCA

NaCCA's official Secondary Education Curriculum page lists the current SHS 1–3 subject curricula, including General Science, Physics, Mathematics, Computing, Robotics, Engineering, and Art and Design. Prefer these official curriculum PDFs for subject-level alignment.

- [NaCCA Secondary Education Curriculum documents](https://nacca.gov.gh/secondary-education-curriculum/)
- [NaCCA curriculum development and Key Phase 5 overview](https://nacca.gov.gh/curriculum/)
- [NaCCA secondary subject-combination guidelines](https://nacca.gov.gh/subject-combination-guidelines-secondary-education/)
- [General Science curriculum for SHS 1–3 (NaCCA PDF)](https://nacca.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/GENERAL-SCIENCE-Curriculum.pdf)
- [Physics curriculum for SHS 1–3 (NaCCA PDF)](https://nacca.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Physics-Curriculum.pdf)
- [Robotics curriculum for SHS 1–3 (NaCCA PDF)](https://nacca.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ROBOTICS-Curriculum.pdf)
- [Teacher Assessment Manual and Toolkit (Ministry/NaCCA PDF)](https://curriculumresources.edu.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Teacher-Assessment-Manual-and-Toolkit-_Handbook-for-Teachers.pdf)

The official SHS curricula describe learning across three years using content standards, learning indicators, teaching exemplars, and assessment strategies. General Science includes strands on materials, living processes, energy, forces and mechanisms, consumer electronics, health, and technology in local industries. Use the full document to confirm the year, strand, and indicator for a specific answer.

NaCCA describes SHS as Key Phase 5 and the program as learner-centred, with STEM connections across different learning areas. Subject combinations can depend on school focus and capacity, so never assume a learner takes every STEAM subject.

The assessment manual is teacher-facing. Its overview covers formative, summative, and diagnostic assessment, differentiation, fairness, inclusion, and e-assessment. Use it later for teacher support, not as a student curriculum source.

NaCCA curriculum PDFs may carry restrictions on reproduction. For this prototype, keep links and bibliographic metadata, provide original explanations, and do not copy or redistribute substantial passages or whole documents. Confirm permission before bulk ingestion or republishing source text.

## WASSCE practice and examiner guidance

Use these online resources for school-candidate and private-candidate preparation, including November/December practice. Ask which candidate session the learner is preparing for when it matters. Exam schedules can change, so check WAEC's current timetable. Make clear whether an item is an original past question or a newly written WASSCE-style question. Third-party practice sites are independent and are not WAEC; check candidate type, subject, exam year, paper, and answer explanation before relying on an item. Link learners to the original page rather than copying a whole paper or question bank into Lagaa.

- [WAEC Ghana Chief Examiners' Reports](https://waecgh.org/chief-examiners-report/) — official examiner feedback and subject-specific guidance; useful for identifying common errors, but not a complete past-question bank.
- [WAEC Ghana timetables](https://waecgh.org/timetable/) — official, current schedules for school-candidate and private-candidate exam series.
- [WAEC e-Learning](https://www.waeconline.org.ng/e-learning/) — official WAEC regional study resource with subject pages containing selected past WASSCE questions and examiner feedback; some pages list school and private-candidate series, including Nov/Dec. Coverage varies by subject and year, and it is not established here as a complete Ghana-only archive. Use for practice and examiner insight, and direct learners to the original subject page.
- [LMS Ghana past questions](https://www.lmsgh.net/practice/pastQuestion) — an online platform whose use guide is hosted on Ghana's curriculum resources site. The guide shows a subject/year/paper browsing interface for selected subjects and sample years, but the full current collection and access requirements could not be verified. The [platform guide](https://curriculumresources.edu.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LMS-Ghana-Platform-User-Guide-Book-68.pdf) shows how to browse; it does not establish that every subject or sitting is available.
- [Examry practice](https://www.examry.com/practice) — independent objective practice with subject/year filters and solution links. The current public page highlights a subset of recent subjects/years; its listed phone number uses Gambia's country code, so do not assume every paper is Ghana-specific.
- [Study for WASSCE](https://www.studyforwassce.com/landing.php) — independent paper portal. Its current page reports 13 subjects, 128 papers, and four years; terms prohibit automated bulk downloads and redistribution. Its privacy policy describes analytics, IP/server logs, and advertising cookies despite no required student account.
- [Nimdi](https://www.nimdi.com/) — preferred independent discovery source for the requested 1993-to-date range: its site and app listing claim WASSCE June/July and private-candidate Nov/Dec questions arranged by subject/topic from 1993 onward. The collection's completeness and latest-year coverage have not been independently verified; check the selected subject, sitting, paper, and year on the source.
- [SHSMathPro](https://shsmathpro.com/) — Ghana-focused Core Mathematics practice with timed quizzes and worked solutions. Its current sign-up asks for full name, phone number, school, and class; do not make it a default student recommendation because Lagaa requires no personal details.
- [PASSCO Ghana](https://passco.com.gh/) — independent Ghana-oriented past-question service. The site describes USSD access followed by an SMS link/password; coverage and answer accuracy have not been independently verified. Disclose the phone/SMS route before recommending it.
- [Exams Ghana](https://examsghana.com/sssce/) — independent year-organized repository that claims SSSCE from 1993–2005 and WASSCE from 2006 onward. Completeness, answer accuracy, and reuse rights are unverified.
- [Inkwell WASSCE question bank](https://inkwelldirectory.com/past-questions) — independent, source-led bank; the reviewed public page shows a limited Core Mathematics selection (2000–2017), not a full multi-subject archive.
- [WASSCE Practice](https://wasscepractice.com/) — independent practice site; current content, question coverage, privacy, and terms could not be verified during review, so do not recommend until checked.

WAEC's examiner reports are official sources; the practice platforms above are third-party discovery links, not endorsements. Before using or importing any actual past question, check the source's terms and copyright status. Do not claim that a generated item appeared in a past WASSCE paper.

## Optional concept-learning and activity platforms

Use these for a specific concept when their materials fit the Ghana SHS topic. They are supplements, not Ghana curriculum or WASSCE authorities. Prefer a text explanation if data or device access is a concern.

- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) — structured, self-paced explanations and practice with feedback; its scope and course sequence are not a Ghana SHS syllabus.
- [PhET Interactive Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu/) — research-based interactive math/science models. Download/offline options are available for some platforms; use only when a simulation genuinely helps and provide a text alternative.
- [CK-12 / Flexi](https://www.ck12.org/) — digital lessons, practice, and an AI learning assistant. Content may follow non-Ghana standards; its privacy policy describes account/device/activity data, so never send learner chat or identifying information there.
- [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize) — accessible revision material organized around UK curricula/exams. Use for a matching concept only, not as proof of Ghana syllabus coverage or WASSCE format.
